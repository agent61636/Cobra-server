Before your browser can knock on the gate, it has to ask "where is music.songofeo.com?" On Cobra Island, two Pi-holes answer that question for every machine, block the ad and tracker names, and send the rest up to OPNsense.

Neither Pi-hole is a container in this guide's pods. Pi-hole #1 is a small Proxmox container, CT 101 cobra-pihole-1 at 10.6.9.3 on the Latitude. Pi-hole #2 is installed natively on the SGIN at 10.6.9.20, right beside the stream pod. OPNsense's DHCP hands every machine both addresses, so if one goes down for an update, the other keeps answering.

Their best trick is the wildcard: any name under songofeo.com gets answered with 10.6.9.10, the gate. At home your phone never goes out to the internet and back to reach your own server; it walks straight down the hall. That's why the floors open without the sign-in at home: the gate sees a 10.6.9.x address, and Authelia lets home addresses through.

HOW IT WORKSTHREE MAGIC LINES

  
THE DNSMASQ LINES (SAME ON BOTH)

    
    
      
  • address=/*.songofeo.com/10.6.9.10: every name under songofeo.com goes to the gate. The *. leaves the bare songofeo.com alone, so the station on Porkbun's hosting still loads at home.
    • 
      
  • server=/www.songofeo.com/#: www is the station too, so it's passed up to the real DNS.
    • 
      
  • server=/vpn.songofeo.com/#: the WireGuard endpoint. It must be your public address even at home, or a laptop that sets up its tunnel at home would point it at the gate.
    • 
    

  

  

    
SHORT NAMES

      
Both Pi-holes carry the same 8 host entries, each with a full and a short name: cobra-firewall .1, cobra-command .2, cobra-pihole-1 .3, cobra-switch .5, cobra-ap .6, cobra-gate .10, cobra-stream .20, cobra-arcade .30.

      
The short ones matter over WireGuard: a VPN client doesn't get the home.arpa search suffix, so \\cobra-stream would otherwise not resolve.

    

    
WHY "ALL" IS SAFE HERE

      
Pi-hole's default LOCAL mode ignores clients on subnets it has no interface on, and WireGuard's 10.6.99.0/24 is one of those. So both run listeningMode ALL. That's safe because nothing outside reaches them: CT 101 sits only on the LAN, and the SGIN's nftables lets port 53 in from the LAN and WireGuard only.

    

  

  

    
HOW MACHINES FIND THEM

      
OPNsense runs Dnsmasq DNS & DHCP. Its DHCP option 6 (DNS servers) hands out 10.6.9.3, 10.6.9.20, with the domain home.arpa. A machine that renews its lease gets both.

    

    
WHERE THE REST GOES

      
Names that aren't blocked and aren't local go up to OPNsense at 10.6.9.1, whose Unbound resolver sends them out over DNS over TLS. Your ISP sees an encrypted stream, not your lookups.

TIPS & TRICKS

  

    

      
PRO TIP: SYNC THE TWINS

        
Change blocklists on Pi-hole #1, then copy them across: #1 → Settings → Teleporter → Export, and #2 → Teleporter → Import. Do it on the-box.

      

      
PRO TIP: SITE BROKEN?

        
Query Log on either admin page, filter by your machine, reload the site. Blocked names show in red.

      

      
CODES

        THE-BOX · PowerShell
        ipconfig /renew
        ipconfig /all

PRO TIP: XFINITY WI-FI

        
Xfinity's router has no NAT loopback, so songofeo names don't load from the Xfinity Wi-Fi. Use the WireGuard home profile (endpoint 10.0.0.2:51820) until the cobra access point arrives.

      

    

  

  

  

    

    
JOE SABOTAGE REPORT

  

  

    

      
THE GREEDY WILDCARD

        
The wildcard also grabbed vpn., which must stay public for WireGuard.The server=/vpn.songofeo.com/# line on both Pi-holes passes it up.

      

      
THE SILENT DHCP

        
OPNsense's Dnsmasq had no DNS option at all, so every machine got 10.6.9.1 and skipped the Pi-holes.DHCP option 6 = 10.6.9.3, 10.6.9.20.

      

    

    

      
THE STATIC DESKTOP

        
the-box had a hand-typed IP with one DNS server and no suffix, so short names never resolved.Switched to DHCP with a reservation at .50.

      

      
THE VPN THAT EATS DNS

        
PIA VPN on the-box takes over DNS and reconnects by itself, so lookups skip the Pi-holes.Disconnect PIA before testing anything DNS.

      

    

  

  
THE MISSING TWIN ENTRIES

    
Pi-hole #1 was missing cobra-switch and cobra-ap while #2 had all 8, so a name worked or failed depending on which twin your machine asked.Both now carry the same 8 hosts. Change one, change the other the same day (Settings → Local DNS Records, or Teleporter).
