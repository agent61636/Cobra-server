Icecast is the radio tower of Song of Eo Radio. It doesn't pick songs and it doesn't play anything by itself. It takes one live audio stream in from the DJ and hands copies out to every listener who tunes in.

The DJ is the next chapter's container, radio (Liquidsoap), which connects to Icecast with a source password and pushes an endless MP3 stream to the mount point /stream. Listeners open http://10.6.9.20:8000/stream at home, or https://radio.songofeo.com/stream through the gate, in a browser, VLC or a radio app.

Icecast is part of manual part 8, the landing site and radio. It joins the stream pod on port 8000, which needs adding to the pod's published ports and to the SGIN's nftables list. It keeps no data folder at all: everything it knows comes from its settings.

THREE PASSWORDS

        
    
          
  • Source: what the DJ uses to push audio in. The radio container gets the same one.
    • 
          
  • Admin: for Icecast's admin pages (listeners, kicking a source).
    • 
          
  • Relay: for other Icecast servers relaying this one. Unused here, but set.
    • 
        

        
With the scripts they're generated once as podman secrets (icecast-source-password and friends). With the manual's Quadlets they're random values written into icecast.container, chmod 600.

      

      
THROUGH THE GATE

        
Manual 8.3 gives radio. its own nginx server block. location / asks Authelia (password only, one_factor), turns buffering off so audio flows straight through, and allows a listener to stay tuned for 24 hours. location /admin allows only 10.6.9.0/24 and 10.6.99.0/24.

      

      
WHY BEHIND A LOGIN?

        
Streaming music you don't hold the rights to, to anyone on the internet, needs public-performance licences, even for a tiny station. So radio. stays behind the gate's sign-in for you and the people you add. Fully public only with music you have rights to: your own, Creative Commons or royalty-free (manual 8.4).

      

    

    

      
TIPS & TRICKS

      
PRO TIP: LAN TEST

        
On the-box, open http://10.6.9.20:8000/stream in VLC (Media → Open Network Stream) or the browser. Music = Icecast and the DJ both work. Silence or 404 = the DJ isn't connected.

      

      
PRO TIP: THE STATUS PAGE

        
http://10.6.9.20:8000/ shows Icecast's status page: the mount, what's playing and how many are listening.

      

      
CODES

        SGIN · podsvc
        podman logs --tail 30 icecast
        podman restart icecast
        The DJ reconnects by itself.
        podman secret inspect --showsecret --format '{{.SecretData}}' icecast-admin-password
        Scripts mode: the admin password, for /admin.
        SGIN · eels
        sudo nft list ruleset | grep 8000
        8000 must be in the LAN/VPN port list, or the gate can't reach Icecast.
      

      
PRO TIP: UPLOAD MATH

        
Each listener at 192 kb/s uses about 0.2 Mb/s of Xfinity upload. Ten listeners, about 2 Mb/s.

JOE SABOTAGE REPORT

      
From manual part 8 and the stream pod's port rules.

    

  

  

    

      
THE PIRATE STATION

        
Removing the sign-in from radio. turns a private jukebox into a public broadcast of music you may not have the rights to stream.Keep the Authelia rule unless every track is yours, Creative Commons or royalty-free.

      

      
THE CLOSED FIREWALL

        
Icecast plays fine on the SGIN itself, but radio. through the gate times out. Port 8000 isn't in nftables' list.Part 8.2's sed line adds 8000, then sudo systemctl reload nftables.

      

      
THE UNPUBLISHED PORT

        
Quadlet mode: forget PublishPort=8000:8000 in stream.pod and the LAN can't reach Icecast at all. (pod.sh already has 8000.)Add it under [Pod], daemon-reload, restart stream-pod.

      

    

    

      
THE OPEN CONTROL ROOM

        
Icecast's /admin pages can kick the DJ off the air.nginx allows /admin only from the LAN and WireGuard. Test: from the S21 on cellular, radio.songofeo.com/admin/ must be refused.

      

      
THE PASSWORD MISMATCH

        
Change Icecast's source password but not the radio container's, and the DJ is locked out: dead air.Both read the same secret (scripts) or the same value (Quadlets). Change both, restart both.

      

      
THE FULL HOUSE

        
Listener 51 gets turned away: ICECAST_MAX_CLIENTS=50. Your upload speed runs out long before that matters.Raise it only if the upload can carry it.
