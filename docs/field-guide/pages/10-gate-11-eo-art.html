eo-art is the station's picture hanger. When someone opens a Song of Eo landing page, the page asks eo-art for a picture, and eo-art hands back one random image from the right folder: a movie backdrop for the Cinema, album covers for Music, book, comic and game-book covers for the Library, Comics and the Game Cellar.

It's a single standard-library Python file, eo_art.py, from the Project-Eo repository (home/eo-art/eo_art.py). The stock python:3-slim image runs it from /srv/gate/eo-art, mounted read-only, on 127.0.0.1:8790 inside the gate pod.

The pictures come from the SGIN over the CIFS mount: dock1a/art, with five flat folders of .jpg copies. eo-art only reads them, and rescans every two minutes. An empty folder simply means that page shows its own drawn artwork instead.

A FLOOR, NOT A ROOM

        
art.songofeo.com is in nginx's $station map, next to music. and books., and in Authelia's floor rule. So it opens straight away at home and over WireGuard, and asks for one password elsewhere. Signed in on the station, the landing pages' pictures just load.

      

      
NO PUBLIC NAME (YET)

        
Manual 5.5 gives art no Porkbun record for now. At home and over WireGuard the Pi-holes answer it; from outside it doesn't resolve, so visitors there see each page's drawn artwork instead of live pictures.

      

      
THE PLAYERS' CORS FILE

        
The station's pages live on Porkbun's hosting and call the floors from there, so nginx answers them with CORS headers from station-cors.inc. That file came from Project-Eo (home/nginx/station-cors.inc) with eo_art.py.

      

    

    

      
RESCANS ITSELF

        
eo-art looks at its five folders every two minutes. Drop new pictures in and they join the rotation without a restart.

      

      
404 IS FINE

        
An empty folder answers 404, and the page falls back to its drawn art. The run script's health check counts any HTTP answer, 404 included, as "up".

      

      
NO CURL INSIDE

        
python:3-slim has no curl or wget, so health checks ask with Python's urllib from inside the container.

      

      
READ-ONLY EVERYTHING

        
The script folder is mounted :ro and the pictures are mounted read-only. eo-art can't change a single file, on the gate or on the SGIN.

      

    

  

    

    
EO-ART ANSWERSTHE PAGE SHOWS

    
A pictureYour picture, picked at random from that folder

    
404 (folder empty)The page's own drawn artwork

    
Sign-in redirect (outside, not signed in)Drawn artwork until you sign in on the station

    
Nothing (eo-art down)Drawn artwork; nginx logs a 502

  



  
WHEN THE SGIN NAPS

    
Like Paperless and MeTube, eo-art needs the SGIN's share. Its Quadlet has Restart=on-failure every 60 seconds, so it comes back once the share does, and up-all.sh lists it as "did not start" without holding up the rest of the gate.

  






  
TIPS & TRICKSPOWER-UPS

  

    

      
PRO TIP: HANG PICTURES

        
On THE-BOX, open \\cobra-stream\media\dock1a\art in Explorer and copy .jpg files into the five folders. Copies, not your library: eo-art shows whatever's there.

      

      
PRO TIP: WIDE FOR CINEMA

        
Backdrops fill a wide page: pick landscape pictures for movies. Covers for the other four can be portrait.

      

      
PRO TIP: SEE ONE NOW

        
On THE-BOX's browser, open https://art.songofeo.com/cinema/backdrop. Reload for a new one. 404 means the movies folder is still empty.

      

      
PRO TIP: KEEP IT LIGHT

        
Every visit downloads a picture. A few hundred KB each keeps the pages snappy on a phone; there's no need for 20 MB originals.

JOE SABOTAGE REPORT

      
The gallery's empty frames, and how they got that way.

    

  

  

    

      
THE MISSING SCRIPT

        
eo_art.py lives in the Project-Eo repository, not in cobra-server, so the gate had no copy and eo-art wouldn't start. art. failed check.sh.Copied from the Project-Eo ZIP on THE-BOX by scp on 6 Nov, 01:38Z. Same for station-cors.inc.

      

      
THE MISSING FOLDERS

        
The run script stops if any of the five art folders doesn't exist.As eels on the gate: mkdir -p /srv/storage/disks/dock1a/art/{movies,albums,books,comics,gaming}.

      

      
THE WRONG FORMAT

        
The art folders are meant for flat sets of .jpg files. Subfolders or other formats may simply be skipped.Flat folders of .jpg copies.

      

    

    

      
THE BLANK OUTSIDE

        
On cellular the landing pages show drawn art, not your pictures. That's because art. has no public DNS record yet.Expected. Add a CNAME for art later if you want live pictures from outside.

      

      
THE LOST HEADER

        
station-cors.inc uses add_header, which can silently drop the HSTS header on the floors.Test with curl.exe -sI https://tv.songofeo.com/ and add the HSTS line to the .inc if it's missing (manual 5.4).

      

      
THE SHARE DOWN

        
No SGIN share, no pictures, and eo-art restart-loops every minute.Fix the CIFS mount first (ls /srv/storage/disks as eels).
