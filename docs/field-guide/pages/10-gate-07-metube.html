MeTube is a web page with one box: paste a video link, pick a quality, press Add. Behind it, the famous downloader yt-dlp fetches the video and saves it into the library's youtube folder, where Jellyfin can show it on every TV in the house.

It runs in the gate pod on 127.0.0.1:8081, behind nginx and Authelia's two-factor sign-in at metube.songofeo.com. It's an admin room: anyone who could reach it could fill your drives.

The finished videos don't stay on the gate. They go over the CIFS mount to the SGIN's wd2/youtube, a folder on the Seagate's spare space that appears as /media/wd2/youtube to Jellyfin. MeTube's own queue, history and half-finished downloads stay on the gate in /srv/gate/metube, so a big download doesn't trickle over the network a piece at a time.

YT-DLP INSIDE

        
MeTube is a friendly face on yt-dlp, the command-line downloader. yt-dlp does the real work: finds the video's streams, picks the quality you asked for, downloads, and joins sound and picture into one file.

      

      
WHY IT AGES FAST

        
Video sites change their pages often, and yt-dlp ships fixes to keep up. A MeTube image a few weeks old can suddenly fail on every link. The cure is a newer image: that's why the run script ends with "yt-dlp ages fast".

      

      
WEBSOCKETS

        
The progress bars update live over a websocket. nginx keeps those open (proxy_read_timeout 1h on the admin block), so a long download doesn't freeze the page.

      

    

    

      
WHEN THE SGIN IS OFF

        
No SGIN, no wd2/youtube, and MeTube can't start. Its Quadlet has Restart=on-failure every 60 seconds, so it comes back by itself once the share does. up-all.sh also lists it as "did not start" without stopping the other apps.

      

      
THE WRITE PERMISSION

        
MeTube runs as a non-root user inside its container, an unnamed ID on the gate. The CIFS mount uses file_mode=0666,dir_mode=0777 so that user may write. On the SGIN, the files still land as user gate, group media.

      

      
NOT BACKED UP

        
The Seagate's wd2 folder is spare space lent to the library. It's on the same drive as the backups, so it isn't backed up. Fine for videos you could download again.

TIPS & TRICKSPOWER-UPS

  

    
      
PRO TIP: QUEUE IS SAFE

        
The queue and history live in /srv/gate/metube. Restart the container mid-download and the queue is still there afterwards.

      


      
PRO TIP: JELLYFIN SHELF

        
In Jellyfin (THE-BOX, browser): Dashboard, Libraries, Add. Type Home Videos and Photos, folder /media/wd2/youtube. New downloads show up after the next library scan.

      

      
PRO TIP: AUDIO ONLY

        
Pick an audio format in the quality menu to grab just the sound: talks, live sets, a podcast episode. Then move the file somewhere a Navidrome can see it.

      

      
PRO TIP: WHOLE PLAYLISTS

        
Paste a playlist link and MeTube queues every video in it. Big playlists take a while; the queue survives a restart.

      

      
PRO TIP: THE BOOKMARKLET

        
MeTube's project README has a bookmarklet. Add it to THE-BOX's bookmarks bar (with your metube. address) and one click sends the video you're watching to the queue.

      

    

    

      
PRO TIP: FIX "IT STOPPED"

        
Every link failing at once? It's almost always an old yt-dlp. Pull the newest image and restart.

      

      
CODES

        GATE · podsvc
        podman pull ghcr.io/alexta69/metube:latest
        systemctl --user restart metube
        podman logs --tail 40 metube
        yt-dlp's errors say what went wrong.
      

      
PRO TIP: WATCH THE SPACE

        
Videos add up fast. Check how full the Seagate is before a playlist binge.

      

      
CODES

        GATE · eels
        df -h /srv/storage/disks/wd2
        du -sh /srv/storage/disks/wd2/youtube
      

      
PRO TIP: CHANNELS?

        
MeTube is for one-off grabs. To follow channels automatically, the research notes point at a different app (Pinchflat).

      

    

  

  

  
KEEP IT FRESH

    
The weekly routine from manual 5.7 covers MeTube: as podsvc, podman auto-update --dry-run lists images with a newer version, and podman auto-update pulls them and restarts those containers, rolling back if one fails to start. MeTube's Quadlet has AutoUpdate=registry, so it's included.

  

  
PRO TIP: SORT INTO SHOWS

    
A channel you grab often can get its own subfolder in wd2/youtube (make it in Explorer on THE-BOX, then move the files in). Jellyfin's Home Videos library shows each folder as its own shelf.

  






  

    

    

      
JOE SABOTAGE REPORT

      
The tape deck jams in a few predictable ways.

    

  

  

    

      
THE STALE DOWNLOADER

        
Weeks without an update and every download fails with an extraction error.Pull and restart (codes, previous page). The weekly podman auto-update catches it too.

      

      
THE MISSING SHARE

        
The SGIN reboots or the gate's Samba password is wrong: MeTube can't find wd2/youtube and keeps restarting.As eels: ls /srv/storage/disks/wd2. Empty or an error means fix the CIFS mount first.

      

      
THE STACKED FSTAB

        
The CIFS mount line got pasted eight times into /etc/fstab on setup night.One line only: grep -n srv/storage/disks /etc/fstab must show exactly one.

      

    

    

      
THE FULL DRIVE

        
A runaway playlist can fill the Seagate, which also holds the PBS backups.Check df -h before big grabs and prune old videos.

      

      
THE INVISIBLE VIDEOS

        
Downloads finished but Jellyfin shows nothing: no library points at the folder yet, or no scan has run.Add the Home Videos library, then Scan All Libraries.

      

      
THE OPEN DECK

        
MeTube has no login of its own. Without Authelia in front, anyone could fill your drives.metube. stays in the two_factor rule and its server block keeps auth_request.

      

    

  

    

    
THE GATE DISK SQUEEZE

      
Partial downloads sit on the gate's 64 GB disk until they finish. A giant 4K grab can crowd it.Grab huge files one at a time, and check df -h /srv/gate.

    

    
THE ODD FILE NAMES

      
Video titles become file names, emoji and all. Jellyfin copes, but sorting can look messy.Rename in Explorer on THE-BOX via \\cobra-stream if it bothers you.
