Vaultwarden is your own password manager server. It speaks the Bitwarden language, so the official Bitwarden apps, browser extensions and desktop client all work with it, but the encrypted vault lives on the gate, not in someone else's cloud.

Your passwords are encrypted on your device with your master password before they ever reach the server. The gate only stores scrambled blobs. Even with full access to /srv/gate/vaultwarden, nobody can read a single entry without that master password. That's also why there's no "reset my master password" button: lose it and the vault is gone.

Vaultwarden is the one gate app that never goes through Authelia. Instead, nginx refuses everyone who isn't on the LAN (10.6.9.0/24) or WireGuard (10.6.99.0/24) with a plain 403. From the internet, the vault doesn't even have a public DNS name: there's deliberately no vault CNAME in Porkbun.

ZERO KNOWLEDGE

        
Your master password never leaves your device. The app turns it into a key, unlocks the vault locally, and sends only encrypted items to the server. Vaultwarden stores them in db.sqlite3 and hands them back on sync.

      

      
THE SIGN-UP SWITCH

        
SIGNUPS_ALLOWED decides if strangers who reach the page can make an account. It was true just long enough for the Commander to create his account on 6 November, then switched to false.

        
In the Quadlet setup it's a line in vaultwarden.container. The run script does it by itself: open until db.sqlite3 exists, closed on the next run.

      

      
DOMAIN

        
DOMAIN=https://vault.songofeo.com tells Vaultwarden its own address, so links and the web vault work behind nginx.

      

    

    

      
OFFLINE COPIES

        
The Bitwarden apps keep an encrypted copy of your vault on the device. Away from home without WireGuard, you can still unlock and read what was last synced. New entries sync the next time the app reaches the server.

      

      
WHY NOT AUTHELIA?

        
The Bitwarden apps talk to the server in the background with their own tokens. They can't stop to show a web sign-in page. A network rule (LAN and WireGuard only) is simpler and keeps the vault off the internet entirely.

      

      
WHERE IT STARTED

        
Before the vault existed, passwords lived in KeePassXC (manual part 0.1, "a safe place for secrets"). The manual's checklist imports that interim database into Vaultwarden once the account exists.

      

      
THE ADMIN PAGE

        
Vaultwarden's /admin page only switches on when an ADMIN_TOKEN is set. Ours isn't, so the page stays off. One less door to guard.

      

    

  

  

    
IN /SRV/GATE/VAULTWARDENWHAT IT IS

    
db.sqlite3Accounts and every encrypted item. Its existence is what tells the run script to close sign-ups.

    
attachments/Files you attach to entries, encrypted the same way

    
rsa_key*The server's own signing key for login tokens. Lose it and everyone just logs in again.

TIPS & TRICKSPOWER-UPS

  

    

      
PRO TIP: POINT THE APP

        
In any Bitwarden app or extension, before logging in pick Self-hosted (the region menu) and set the server URL to https://vault.songofeo.com. Do the browser extension on THE-BOX first; the phone app next, with WireGuard on.

      

      
PRO TIP: TOTP INSIDE

        
The Bitwarden app can act as your authenticator for Authelia's TOTP codes too. Keep a second copy in Aegis or 2FAS, so one lost phone doesn't lock you out of both.

      

      
PRO TIP: STAY UNLOCKED

        
Set the app's vault timeout to something you'll live with and turn on fingerprint unlock. A strong master password you type once a day beats a weak one you type often.

      

      
CODES

        GATE · podsvc
        podman logs --tail 30 vaultwarden
        grep SIGNUPS ~/.config/containers/systemd/vaultwarden.container
        Must say SIGNUPS_ALLOWED=false.
        systemctl --user restart vaultwarden
      

    

    

      
PRO TIP: SHARE SAFELY

        
Vaultwarden supports Bitwarden organizations: make one for the house and share the streaming, Wi-Fi and utility logins with Rachel, while your own items stay private.

      

      
PRO TIP: GENERATE IT

        
Every Bitwarden app has a password generator. Use it for every new app on the island (Uptime Kuma's admin, Kavita, RomM, the friends' Jellyfin accounts) and save the entry right there.

      

      
PRO TIP: ALIVE CHECK

        
Vaultwarden answers /alive when it's healthy. The run script waits on it, and Homepage's tile watches http://127.0.0.1:8222.

      

      
CODES

        GATE · podsvc
        podman exec nginx curl -s http://127.0.0.1:8222/alive
        Prints a timestamp when it's up (nginx's image has curl).
      

    

  

  

    
PRO TIP: EMERGENCY SHEET

      
Print one page for the house safe: the master password, the server URL https://vault.songofeo.com, and "turn on WireGuard first". Future you will thank you.

    

    
PRO TIP: HEALTH REPORT

      
The Bitwarden web vault and apps can flag reused and weak passwords. Run it after the KeePassXC import and fix the worst ones first.

    

  

  

  
BACKUPS

    
Everything Vaultwarden knows is in /srv/gate/vaultwarden (the database plus attachments), inside VM 110, so the nightly Proxmox backup to PBS at 02:00 carries it. For an extra copy you control, the Bitwarden apps can export the vault as an encrypted file from THE-BOX. Store it off the gate.

  






  

    

    

      
JOE SABOTAGE REPORT

      
Found in the footlockers, or flagged in manual part 5.4. The vault has few traps, but each one is expensive.

    

  

  

    

      
THE OPEN DOOR

        
The manual's Quadlet ships with SIGNUPS_ALLOWED=true so you can make the first account. Leave it, and anyone on the LAN can make one too.Closed on 6 Nov. After any rebuild from the manual, close it again: sed the line to false, daemon-reload, restart.

      

      
THE PUBLIC NAME

        
A Porkbun CNAME for vault would advertise it to the world, even though nginx refuses outsiders.No public vault record. The Pi-holes answer it at home and over WireGuard.

      

    

    

      
THE FORGOTTEN MASTER

        
There is no master password reset. The server can't decrypt your vault, so it can't help.Write the master password on paper and keep it somewhere safe in the house.

      

      
THE XFINITY DEAD END

        
On the Xfinity Wi-Fi, songofeo.com names point at the public address and Xfinity has no NAT loopback.Use the "home" WireGuard profile (endpoint 10.0.0.2:51820), or cellular plus WireGuard.

      

    

  

  

    
THE LONE COPY

      
If the only device with your vault is the phone, and the gate dies, you're down to one encrypted copy.Log the browser extension on THE-BOX in too, and keep the nightly VM backup.

    

    
THE PLAIN EXPORT

      
Bitwarden can also export an unencrypted file. That's every password in plain text.Pick the encrypted export format, and never leave an export in Downloads.

    

  

  

    
THE PHONE-ONLY 2FA

      
If Authelia's TOTP lives only in the Bitwarden app, and the vault won't sync, you can't reach the admin rooms to fix it.Keep the TOTP seeds in a second app too.

    

    
THE REBUILD RESET

      
Running vaultwarden.sh with SIGNUPS_ALLOWED=true on purpose reopens the door, and it stays open.Run it again without the variable: the database exists, so sign-ups close.
