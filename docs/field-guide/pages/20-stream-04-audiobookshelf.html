Audiobookshelf (ABS to its friends) is the Library floor. It turns folders of audiobook files into a proper bookshelf with covers, chapters, series and narrators, and remembers exactly where every listener stopped, down to the second, across every device.

It also shelves e-books in a second library, with a reader in the browser. Audiobooks are where it shines: nothing else self-hosted comes close for audio, and its phone app can download books for offline listening on a long drive.

ABS lives in the stream pod on port 13378. Its own settings and its database sit in /srv/stream/abs/config, and the covers and caches it makes in /srv/stream/abs/metadata, both on the SGIN's SSD. The books come in read-only from dock1a.

WHY PORT 13378?

        
On its own, ABS listens on port 80. Inside a pod every container shares one network, so two apps can't both have the same port, and a rootless container can't have 80 anyway. The container is started with PORT=13378, and the pod publishes 13378 to the LAN.

      

      
HOW IT READS A BOOK

        
ABS treats each folder as one book. A tidy layout is Author / Book Title / files, with a series folder in between if you like: Author / Series / Book 1 Title / files. One file or fifty MP3 parts, it's all one book to ABS, and chapters come from the file or the parts.

        
It then looks up covers, descriptions and narrators from online sources and saves what it finds in its own metadata folder, because the library is read-only.

THE TWO LOCKS

        
From outside, books. is a station floor: the gate's sign-in first, then ABS's own login. At home and over WireGuard the gate skips its sign-in, and you meet ABS's login directly. The ABS login is what keeps each listener's progress their own.

      

      
WHO'S THE ROOT?

        
The very first visit creates the root user, the top admin. Everyone else gets a normal user under Settings → Users.

PRO TIP: THE PHONE APP

        
Install the Audiobookshelf app, server https://books.songofeo.com, your ABS user. Away from home, turn WireGuard on first: phone apps can't show the gate's sign-in page. Downloaded books play offline anywhere.

      

      
PRO TIP: ADDING BOOKS

        
From the-box, copy the book's folder into \\cobra-stream\media\dock1a\audiobooks\Author\. ABS spots it and shelves it. If not, the library's menu has a scan button.

      

    

    

      
PRO TIP: FIX A COVER

        
Wrong cover or blurb? Open the book → edit (pencil) → Match, and pick the right edition. The fix is stored in ABS's metadata, not the files.

      

      
PRO TIP: SERIES ORDER

        
Put the series name and number in the book's details (or in the folder name, like Book 1 - Title) and ABS lines the series up in reading order on its own shelf.

      

      
PRO TIP: SLEEP TIMER

        
The player has a playback-speed control and a sleep timer. Bedtime stories, Cobra style.

JOE SABOTAGE REPORT

      
From manual 3.8 and the stream pod's wiring.

    

  

  

    

      
THE ROOT GRAB

        
The first person to open ABS becomes root. Leave it unclaimed and that could be anyone on the LAN.Create root from the-box as soon as the container first starts.

      

      
THE PORT 80 HABIT

        
Guides on the internet run ABS on port 80. In the shared pod, and rootless, it can't have 80.Keep PORT=13378 in the container and 13378 in pod.sh and nftables.

      

      
THE UPLOAD WALL

        
The library is mounted read-only, so anything that writes into the book folders, like the web Upload page or saving covers next to the files, can't.Add books by copying them over the share from the-box. ABS keeps its own covers in its metadata folder.

      

      
THE LOOSE FILES

        
Fifty MP3s dumped straight into audiobooks/ become fifty "books", or one mess.One folder per book: Author / Title / files.

      

    

    

      
THE GHOST DRIVE

        
dock1a unplugged and plugged back: ABS still sees the empty folder from before, and its library looks gone.podman pod restart stream, then scan the library.

      

      
THE WRONG TYPE

        
ABS libraries are either Book or Podcast. Pick Podcast for audiobooks and nothing lines up.Both libraries here are type Book.

      

      
THE SHARED ACCOUNT

        
Two people on one ABS user overwrite each other's progress: you'll resume at chapter 20 of a book you were on chapter 3 of.One ABS user per listener.

      

      
THE DOUBLE LOGIN SURPRISE

        
From outside you sign in at the gate, then ABS asks again. That's normal: ABS doesn't read the gate's sign-in the way Navidrome does.Nothing to fix in the browser. For the phone app away from home, use WireGuard, which skips the gate's sign-in.
