Navidrome is a small, fast music server. Point it at a folder of songs and it reads their tags, builds an album and artist library, and plays it in a web player or any Subsonic app on your phone. Cobra Island runs one of it.

Since version 0.58 one Navidrome can hold several libraries, and the admin ticks which libraries each user may see. The Commander's music is library Eels, Rachel's is library Rachel. Rachel's account only sees hers; the admin sees both. Her music never lands in your shuffle unless you pick her shelf too.

Two names lead to the same jukebox: music. and mustang.. Stand-up and The Ward moved to Audiobookshelf, which remembers where you stopped in a long track (the last two pages of this chapter).

WHERE IT CAME FROM
Four separate Navidromes (PR #46) and the N.A.V. clones (PR #52) were folded into one on 8 November 2026, the Commander's call. The old databases are kept in a backup.

  

  
CDS IN, MUSIC OUT

    
Navidrome plays ripped music; it doesn't rip CDs. The path from a disc to your phone: rip it on the-box, fix the tags with MusicBrainz Picard, copy the album folder into music\eels or music\rachel on \\cobra-stream\media\dock1a, and Navidrome finds it on its next scan.

ONE SIGN-IN, NOT TWO

        
Navidrome runs with ND_EXTAUTH_TRUSTEDSOURCES=10.6.9.10/32 (the old name ND_REVERSEPROXYWHITELIST still maps to it): "if a request comes from the gate and carries a Remote-User header, that person is already signed in." Authelia fills in the header, so Navidrome lets you straight in. It only believes that header from 10.6.9.10, and nginx always wipes any Remote-User header a visitor sends.

      

      
THE MUSIC WALL

        
Navidrome makes an account for anyone the gate signs in. So Authelia has a wall in front of music. and mustang.: eels, rachel, and the group music get through, everyone else gets forbidden. tv. never asks Authelia, so a Jellyfin test account is not on this wall. gate-music-one.sh writes it. Put groups: [music] on the person in the Authelia users file, same username.

      

    

    

      
SHELVES DON'T OVERLAP

        
Navidrome won't let one library sit inside another. That's why the main shelf is music/eels and not all of music/: then music/rachel can be a shelf of its own. A person sees whole shelves, never single folders inside one.

      

      
WHY ONE AND NOT FOUR?

        
Four Navidromes don't serve four people any faster. One already streams to many at once; playing a song is just reading a file, and a transcode costs the same CPU in any container. Extra instances only add RAM, another scan of the same disk, and another password per person.

      

    

  

  
WHAT EACH PERSON KEEPS

    
Stars, ratings, playlists and play counts belong to each user. Phone apps only get the shelves that user may see, and search, albums and artists are filtered the same way.

  

  

    
CLAUDE-VIPERMake Rachel's account yourself before she opens it, with only her shelf ticked. Then her first look is already the right one.

  

  

    
WHAT'S IN THE DATA FOLDER

      
/srv/stream/navidrome holds the database: users, shelves, playlists, stars and play counts, plus a cache of covers. Back that up; the music itself lives on dock1a.

    

    
WHAT THE GATE SEES

      
nginx's $station map sends music. and mustang. to :4533, and comedy. and sessions. to Audiobookshelf on :13378, all on 10.6.9.20. One server block for all of them.

    

  






  
TIPS & TRICKSPOWER-UPS

  

    

      
PRO TIP: PHONE APPS

        
Navidrome speaks Subsonic, so any Subsonic app works: Symfonium or Substreamer on the phone, Feishin on the desktop. Server https://music.songofeo.com, your Navidrome username and password.

      

      
PRO TIP: THE APP PASSWORD

        
An account the gate made has a random password. Set a real one once: Settings → Users → the person → Change password. The app uses that on /rest/.

      

      
PRO TIP: CLEAN TAGS

        
Fix tags on the-box before copying music in. MusicBrainz Picard matches albums and writes artist, album, track numbers and cover art.

      

      
PRO TIP: PICK YOUR SHELVES

        
The library picker in the sidebar switches between shelves, or shows them together. The admin sees every shelf.

      

      
PRO TIP: CELLULAR DATA

        
Many Subsonic apps let you pick a lower bitrate on mobile data; Navidrome converts on the fly. Or download albums at home for offline play.

JOE SABOTAGE REPORT

      
From manual 3.8, part 5.4 and the merge of November 2026 (docs/music.md).

    

  

  

    

      
THE FIRST-VISITOR CROWN

        
A brand-new Navidrome makes whoever opens it first the admin.Open it from the-box right after it starts and create eels, the same username as in Authelia.

      

      
THE NAME MISMATCH

        
Authelia says "rachel", Navidrome only knows "Rachel2". The trusted header points at nobody.One username per person, spelled the same in users.yml and in Navidrome.

      

      
THE OVERLAPPING SHELF

        
Library 1 still reads all of music/, so Navidrome refuses music/rachel as a second shelf.Library 1's path must be /media/dock1a/music/eels (sgin-music-one.sh sets it).

      

      
THE OPEN SHELF

        
A shelf marked default for new users lands in every new account, Rachel's included.Untick it on both shelves and tick shelves per person on the Users page.

      

    

    

      
THE MISSING WALL

        
Without the music wall, any friend with a gate login who opens music. gets a Navidrome account of their own.Run gate-music-one.sh; gate-sso-check.sh says who's on the wall.

      

      
THE READ-ONLY SURPRISE

        
Navidrome can't fix a wrong tag or delete a duplicate: its library is mounted read-only.Fix files on the-box over the share (Picard); Navidrome picks the change up on its next scan.

      

      
THE STAND-UP SHUFFLE

        
A comedy album dropped into music/ turns up in the Commander's shuffle.Stand-up goes in dock1a/comedy, podcasts in dock1a/sessions: Audiobookshelf's shelves.

      

      
THE WATCHER LIMIT

        
Navidrome, Jellyfin, Kavita and ABS all watch the library, and Linux's default limit on file watchers is low.Part 3.3 raises it in /etc/sysctl.d/90-inotify.conf. Keep that file.

      

    

  

  
THE WIDE-OPEN WHITELIST

    
The trusted-header setting is the master key. Widen ND_EXTAUTH_TRUSTEDSOURCES (or the old ND_REVERSEPROXYWHITELIST) to the whole LAN and any device at home could claim to be any user.Keep it at exactly 10.6.9.10/32, the gate and nothing else.

ON AIR

    

    

      
The Ward is where your own recordings live: one folder per show, one file per episode. As a Podcast library, each episode is marked played or not, per listener, and resumes where you stopped.

      
Audiobookshelf can also Open RSS feed on a show, so The Ward can be followed in any podcast app with the feed's address.

    

  

  

    
PRO TIP: DATE FIRST
Start each file name with the date, 2026-11-08 Episode title.mp3, so episodes sort in order.

    
PRO TIP: COVER ART
A square cover.jpg in each show folder gives every show its own tile.
