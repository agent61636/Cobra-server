Navidrome is a small, fast music server. Point it at a folder of songs and it reads their tags, builds an album and artist library, and plays it in a web player or any Subsonic app on your phone. Cobra Island runs four of them.

That's on purpose. The island's rule is one Navidrome instance per collection, never one instance with several libraries. Each collection gets its own container, its own port, its own database, its own users and its own name on the gate. Rachel's music never shows up in a comedy search, stand-up never lands in a music shuffle, and your recorded podcasts stay in their own room.

All four use the same image and the same settings; only the name, the port and the folder change. Each one is tiny, about 50 to 150 MB of RAM, so four of them cost less than one Jellyfin transcode.

WHERE THEY CAME FROM
One instance per collection was the Commander's call on 6 November 2026 (PR #46). The scripts and the manual both build all four.

  

  
CDS IN, MUSIC OUT

    
Navidrome plays ripped music; it doesn't rip CDs. The path from a disc to your phone: rip it on the-box, fix the tags with MusicBrainz Picard, copy the album folder into the right collection on \cobra-stream\media\dock1a, and the matching Navidrome finds it on its next scan.

ONE SIGN-IN, NOT TWO

        
Every Navidrome runs with ND_REVERSEPROXYWHITELIST=10.6.9.10/32. That tells it: "if a request comes from the gate and carries a Remote-User header, that person is already signed in." Authelia fills in the header after the gate's sign-in, so Navidrome lets you straight in.

        
Two safety locks: Navidrome only believes that header from 10.6.9.10, and nginx always wipes any Remote-User header a visitor sends themselves. Nobody can type "rachel" into a header and walk in.

      

      
AT HOME IT'S DIFFERENT

        
At home and on WireGuard, the gate skips its sign-in for the floors, so the header is empty. Then Navidrome shows its own login page. That's why each person's username must be the same in Navidrome and in Authelia's users.yml.

      

    

    

      
TAGS, NOT FOLDERS

        
Navidrome builds its library from the tags inside each file: artist, album, track number, year, cover art. Folder names barely matter. Messy tags mean split albums and "Unknown Artist", so tidy tags are the secret to a clean library.

      

      
WHAT EACH ONE SEES

        
The library comes in read-only at /media, and ND_MUSICFOLDER picks the one folder that instance reads. Navidrome scans it on its own and picks up new files. It can play, star, rate and playlist your music, but never change or delete a file.

      

      
THE SETTINGS THEY SHARE

        
    
          
  • ND_PORT: 4533 to 4536, one each (all in one pod, so no two may share)
    • 
          
  • ND_SESSIONTIMEOUT=8h: stay logged in for a long listen
    • 
          
  • ND_ENABLEINSIGHTSCOLLECTOR=false: no anonymous usage reports (scripts)
    • 
          
  • Database and covers cache in /srv/stream/<name> on the SSD
    • 
        

      

    

  

  
WHY NOT ONE BIG ONE?

    
Newer Navidrome can hold several libraries in one instance, and it's tempting. Here it's off the table: separate instances mean separate databases, separate admins, separate play counts and shuffles, and a broken scan in one collection can't touch the others. The Commander likes knowing exactly where everything lives.

  

  

    
CLAUDE-VIPERSame image, same settings, four different folders. Learn one Navidrome and you've learned all four. The four pages after the traps are their character cards.

  

  

    
WHAT'S IN THE DATA FOLDER

      
/srv/stream/navidrome* holds each instance's database: its users, playlists, stars, ratings and play counts, plus a cache of covers. That's the part to back up; the music itself lives on dock1a.

    

    
WHAT THE GATE SEES

      
nginx's $station map sends music. to :4533, comedy. to :4534, mustang. to :4535 and sessions. to :4536, all on 10.6.9.20. Four names, one server block.

    

  






  
TIPS & TRICKSPOWER-UPS

  

    

      
PRO TIP: PHONE APPS

        
Navidrome speaks the Subsonic language, so any Subsonic app works: Symfonium or Substreamer on the phone, Feishin on the desktop. Server address: the floor's name, e.g. https://music.songofeo.com, with your Navidrome username and password.

      

      
PRO TIP: AWAY FROM HOME

        
Phone apps can't show the gate's sign-in page, and from outside the floors always ask for it. Turn on WireGuard on the phone first: then the gate skips its sign-in and the app talks to Navidrome directly.

      

      
PRO TIP: CLEAN TAGS

        
Fix tags on the-box before copying music in. MusicBrainz Picard is the standard tagger: it matches albums and writes artist, album, track numbers and cover art. Then copy into \\cobra-stream\media\dock1a\music\....

      

      
PRO TIP: STAR AND RATE

        
Stars, ratings, playlists and play counts belong to each user in each instance. Rachel's favourites on mustang. are hers alone.

      

      
PRO TIP: CELLULAR DATA

        
Many Subsonic apps let you pick a lower streaming bitrate for mobile data; Navidrome converts on the fly. Or download albums in the app at home for offline play.

JOE SABOTAGE REPORT

      
From manual 3.8, part 5.4 and the four-way split of November 2026.

    

  

  

    

      
THE FIRST-VISITOR CROWN

        
Each new Navidrome makes whoever opens it first the admin. Four instances, four chances to lose the crown.Open each port from the-box right after it starts and create its admin, with the same username as in Authelia.

      

      
THE NAME MISMATCH

        
Authelia says "rachel", Navidrome only knows "Rachel2". The trusted header points at nobody, and the sign-in doesn't carry over.One username per person, spelled the same in users.yml and in every Navidrome.

      

      
THE MERGED LIBRARY

        
"Just add comedy as a second library in the main one, it's easier." It isn't allowed here.New collection = new instance, new port, new name. See the fifth-player moves.

      

      
THE MOVED ROOT

        
The main instance used to read music/eels; now it reads all of music. Until a full scan runs, albums show up twice or go missing.After changing ND_MUSICFOLDER, restart the container and let it rescan.

      

    

    

      
THE HALF-OPENED DOOR

        
A new instance needs its port in four places: the pod, nftables, nginx's map and Authelia. Miss the pod and it never answers; miss nftables and the gate can't reach it; miss Authelia and it's a 403.Do all four, then run check.sh on the gate.

      

      
THE READ-ONLY SURPRISE

        
Navidrome can't fix a wrong tag or delete a duplicate: its library is mounted read-only.Fix files on the-box over the share (Picard), and Navidrome picks the change up on its next scan.

      

      
THE STAND-UP SHUFFLE

        
A comedy album dropped into music/ turns up in the Commander's shuffle halfway through a playlist.Stand-up goes in dock1a/comedy, one folder per album. Podcasts go in dock1a/sessions.

      

      
THE WATCHER LIMIT

        
Four Navidromes, Jellyfin, Kavita and ABS all watch the library for changes, and Linux's default limit on file watchers is low.Part 3.3 raises it in /etc/sysctl.d/90-inotify.conf. Keep that file.

      

    

  

  
THE WIDE-OPEN WHITELIST

    
The trusted-header setting is the master key. Widen ND_REVERSEPROXYWHITELIST to the whole LAN and any device at home could claim to be any user by sending its own Remote-User header.Keep it at exactly 10.6.9.10/32, the gate and nothing else, on all four instances.

LAUGHS

    

    

      
Stand-up albums get a club of their own. A forty-minute set has no business popping up between two songs in a shuffle, and a search for a band shouldn't return a comedian's bit about that band.

      
So comedy lives in dock1a/comedy, one folder per album, and this instance reads only that. It runs on port 4534 and is public at comedy.songofeo.com, behind the same gate sign-in as the other floors.

    

  

  

    
PRO TIP: ALBUM TAGS
Tag each special as an album, with the comedian as album artist. Track per bit if the release has them, or one long track: both play fine.

    
PRO TIP: RESUME
Long tracks are easy to lose your place in. Many Subsonic apps can bookmark or remember the position; check your app's settings.

  

  

    
THE WRONG SHELF
Comedy copied into music/ shows up in the Commander's shuffles and on the radio.Move it to dock1a/comedy; both instances rescan on their own.

ON AIR

    

    

      
The Ward is where your own recordings live: the personal podcasts you've made, one folder per show, each episode tagged like a track on an album.

      
Using Navidrome as the player means every Subsonic app on every phone can play them, with the same login as the rest of the station. They're kept out of the music floor, so an episode never interrupts a playlist.

    

  

  

    
PRO TIP: TAG AS ALBUMS
Show name as the album, you as the album artist, episode number as the track number. Navidrome then lists every show as an album in episode order.

    
PRO TIP: COVER ART
Embed a square cover in each file (Picard or your recording app can), so every show gets its own tile.

  

  

    
THE LOOSE EPISODE
Files with no tags show up as "Unknown Album" in a pile.Tag before copying into dock1a/sessions, one folder per show.
