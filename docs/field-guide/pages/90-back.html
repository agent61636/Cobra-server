RESTART (QUADLETS)

GATE · podsvc systemctl --user restart authelia nginx systemctl --user status paperless systemctl --user restart gate-pod The whole pod, every container in it. systemctl --user daemon-reload After editing any .container file.

PRO TIP: WHERE IT LIVES

Every gate app keeps its own data under /srv/gate/<app>, and the Quadlet files sit in ~/.config/containers/systemd/ as podsvc. Both ride along in the nightly VM backup. The shared files (scans, youtube, art) live on the SGIN's drives and come over the CIFS mount.

CHECK EVERYTHING

GATE · eels or podsvc ~podsvc/cobra-server/scripts/podman/gate/check.sh podman exec nginx nginx -t ss -tlnH Only 22, 80, 443 and 9090 may listen on the gate.

UPDATES

GATE · podsvc podman auto-update --dry-run Which Quadlet images have a newer version (AutoUpdate=registry). podman auto-update Read Authelia's and Paperless's release notes first.

THE SHARE FROM THE SGIN

CERTIFICATE

LETTERS FROM THE BARRACKS
Why do I sign in once on my phone, but my TV never asks?
tv. is the one floor nginx sends straight to Jellyfin without Authelia, because TV apps can't show a web sign-in page. Jellyfin's own login guards it instead.
At home nothing asks me to sign in. Is that a hole?
No. The floors trust home (10.6.9.x) and WireGuard (10.6.99.x) addresses. The admin apps still ask for password and TOTP, even at home.
Can I just open a port to Jellyfin on the SGIN?
Please don't. Everything on the SGIN answers without the gate. Exactly one forward lives on OPNsense: WAN 443 to 10.6.9.10.
How do I give a friend Jellyfin?
On the-box, Jellyfin → Dashboard → Users → add them, with only the libraries you want. Then send them https://tv.songofeo.com. No gate account needed.
The gate rebooted but the SGIN was off. Now what?
Paperless, MeTube and eo-art can't find their shared folder and fail. systemd retries them every minute, so they come back by themselves once the SGIN is up.
Where do my passwords live?
Yours: in Vaultwarden at vault., LAN and WireGuard only. The apps' generated ones: as podman secrets, readable with the codes on the previous page. Never in the scripts.
How do I know when something breaks?
Uptime Kuma at status. watches every app, and Homepage at command. shows a live dot on each tile.
Who gets in through the gate sign-in?
Whoever is in Authelia's users.yml on the gate. Right now that's eels and rachel.
