Authelia is the bouncer nginx calls before letting anyone onto a floor. It owns the sign-in screen at gate.songofeo.com, checks your password, asks admins for a six-digit code, and hands out one cookie that works on every songofeo.com name for six hours.

Nobody visits Authelia on purpose. You tap the gold star Sign in on the station, or open music. from your phone on cellular, and nginx's side request comes back "not signed in". nginx then bounces you to gate.songofeo.com with your page attached as ?rd=. Sign in once and you land back where you started.

It keeps its whole world in one folder, /srv/gate/authelia: the rules in configuration.yml, the people in users.yml (password hashes only, never the passwords), its database db.sqlite3 with everyone's TOTP devices, and notification.txt, where it writes the "emails" it would send.

THE COOKIE

        
Signing in sets authelia_session for the domain songofeo.com, so the bare domain and every *.songofeo.com name share it. It's Secure (https only), HttpOnly (page scripts can't read it) and SameSite Lax.

        
expiration: 6h and inactivity: 6h: six hours after you sign in, it asks again. There's no "remember me" box (remember_me: -1), so every sign-in lasts the same.

      

      
THE HOME BYPASS

        
definitions.network.home is 10.6.9.0/24 (the LAN) and 10.6.99.0/24 (WireGuard). On the floors, a visitor from home walks straight in, so the Navidrome and Audiobookshelf phone apps keep working. They still have their own logins.

        
It trusts the address nginx hands over, which is the real connection address (X-Forwarded-For $remote_addr). That only works because the pod's pasta networking keeps real visitor addresses.

      

    

    

      
TWO SPECIAL CASES

        
tv. never asks Authelia. Friends' smart-TV, Roku and phone Jellyfin apps can't show a web sign-in page, so tv. has its own nginx block with no auth_request, and Jellyfin's own accounts are the lock.

        
vault. never asks Authelia either. nginx simply refuses (403) anyone who isn't on the LAN or WireGuard.

      

      
THE LOCKOUT

        
regulation: 5 wrong passwords within 10 minutes locks that username for 1 hour. It counts per username, so a stranger hammering "eels" locks eels out too. That's the price of keeping guessers out.

      

      
WHERE IT SENDS YOU BACK

        
The rd address must be https and under songofeo.com. Anything else (say example.com) is ignored and you land on the station, songofeo.com. No one can use your sign-in page to bounce people to a fake site.

      

      
WHO NGINX SAYS YOU ARE

        
On the floors, Authelia answers with your username, and nginx passes it on as Remote-User. That's why Music doesn't ask you twice.

BOSS FIGHT: THE TOTP GAUNTLET

  
Every person who opens papers., metube., command. or status. needs a TOTP device registered once. Do the whole fight on THE-BOX. The phone only scans the QR code and reads back six digits.

  

    

      
THE MOVES

        
    
          
  1. THE-BOX, browser: open https://papers.songofeo.com. You get bounced to the dark sign-in screen at gate.
    2. 
          
  2. Sign in with your username and password. Authelia sees the room needs two factors and offers Register device. Pick One-Time Password.
    3. 
          
  3. It says a code was "sent by email". There is no email: the filesystem notifier wrote it into notification.txt on the gate. Read it in your Windows Terminal SSH tab (codes, right).
    4. 
          
  4. Type that code in the browser. A QR code appears on the-box's screen.
    5. 
          
  5. Phone: scan the QR with an authenticator app (Aegis, 2FAS, Google Authenticator, or the Bitwarden app).
    6. 
          
  6. THE-BOX: type the six digits the phone shows. You're in, and Paperless opens.
    7. 
          
  7. Empty the notification file so old codes don't sit around.
    8.

PRO TIP: BACK UP THE SEED

        
Aegis and 2FAS can export an encrypted backup of your codes. Do it right after registering, and keep it somewhere that isn't the phone. A lost phone with no backup means re-registering every room.

      

      
PRO TIP: PASSWORD RESETS

        
"Forgot password?" works the same way: the reset link lands in notification.txt, not in an inbox. Read it with the same tail on the gate.

LETTERS FROM THE BARRACKS

    

      

        
Do I register again for metube. after doing papers.?

        
No. One TOTP device covers every two_factor room. Register once, use it on all four.

        
Rachel only listens to music. Does she need TOTP?

        
No. The floors are one_factor from outside and open at home. TOTP is only for people who use the admin rooms.

      

      

        
The code in notification.txt doesn't work.

        
It's single-use and short-lived. Ask for a new one in the browser, then tail again and use the newest line at the bottom.

        
New phone. Now what?

        
Restore your authenticator backup on the new phone. No backup? Register a new device the same way, from THE-BOX.

      

    

  






  
TIPS & TRICKSPOWER-UPS

  

    

      
PRO TIP: ADD A PLAYER

        
New person? Hash their password and add a block to users.yml with displayname, email and the hash. Use the same username they'll have in Navidrome, so Music signs them in by itself. Then restart Authelia.

      

      
CODES

        GATE · podsvc
        podman run --rm -it docker.io/authelia/authelia:latest authelia crypto hash generate argon2
        Asks for the password without showing it and prints the argon2id digest. Paste only the digest.
        nano /srv/gate/authelia/users.yml
        systemctl --user restart authelia
      

      
PRO TIP: KICK SOMEONE OUT

        
Delete their block from users.yml and restart. Their session ends on the spot.

      

      
PRO TIP: SIGN OUT EVERYWHERE

        
https://gate.songofeo.com/logout ends your session on every name at once. Handy on a borrowed computer.

      

    

    

      
PRO TIP: HEALTH CHECK

        
Authelia has a health page. {"status":"OK"} means it's alive and nginx can ask it.

      

      
CODES

        THE-BOX · PowerShell
        curl.exe -s https://gate.songofeo.com/api/health
        GATE · podsvc
        podman logs --tail 30 authelia
        Every sign-in, failure and lockout is in here.
      

      
PRO TIP: INSPECT THE COOKIE

        
On THE-BOX after signing in: F12, then Application (Chrome) or Storage (Firefox), Cookies, https://gate.songofeo.com. authelia_session should show Domain .songofeo.com, Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite Lax, and expire in six hours.

      

      
PRO TIP: TEST THE BYPASS

        
In a private window on THE-BOX, music. opens straight away (you're home), while papers. still asks for the sign-in and the TOTP code. Same test on cellular: music. must ask first.

      

      
PRO TIP: LONGER SIGN-INS

        
Want 12 hours? Change expiration and inactivity in configuration.yml together, then restart.

JOE SABOTAGE REPORT

      
Stepped on during the build, or flagged in manual part 5.4. This boss bites back.

    

  

  

    

      
THE RESTART PURGE

        
Sessions live in Authelia's memory. Any restart, even to add a user, signs everyone out. Not a bug, just everyone signs in again.Restart when nobody's mid-movie on a floor from outside.

      

      
THE THIRD SECRET

        
configuration.yml holds three random secrets. Change the third one (the storage encryption_key) and Authelia can't read db.sqlite3: everyone's TOTP device is gone.Never touch them. They're in the nightly VM backup with the rest of /srv/gate.

      

      
THE SELF-LOCKOUT

        
Five wrong passwords in ten minutes locks that username for an hour, and a stranger can trigger it for you.Wait the hour, or restart Authelia (which also signs everyone out).

      

      
THE PHONE FUMBLE

        
Registering TOTP on the phone means reading codes off an SSH session on a tiny screen and copy-pasting between apps.THE-BOX does the browser and the SSH tab; the phone only scans the QR.

      

    

    

      
THE CODE PILE

        
notification.txt collects every one-time code and reset link ever sent.Keep it mode 600 and empty it after registering (codes on the boss page).

      

      
THE OLD NAME

        
The portal used to be auth.songofeo.com. That name has no DNS record and no server block any more. Old links to it just fail.Station links point at https://gate.songofeo.com/?rd=.... Fix any leftover auth. in Project-Eo.

      

      
THE GATE CNAME

        
gate is the DDNS A record every other name points at. Adding a CNAME for gate in Porkbun would clash with it.The portal needs no new DNS record. Ever.

      

      
THE OPEN GUEST LIST

        
Every user in users.yml can open every floor, including music. (all the music) and mustang. (Rachel's). This was flagged on 6 November, with per-person rules proposed and waiting on the member list.Until then, only add people you'd hand every floor to.

      

      
THE OLD SPELLING

        
Authelia 4.38 moved named networks to definitions.network. The old access_control.networks only warns today.Read the release notes before podman auto-update pulls a new Authelia.
