You asked how your pod apps work. The Brush-Fangs heard "at least three pages per container, no upper limit" and took it as a direct order. They have not been to the beach since.

      
This issue walks every container on Cobra Island, one chapter each. Two worlds, like any good cartridge. World 1, The Gate, is the little Debian VM cobra-gate at 10.6.9.10 inside the Latitude: the only machine the internet can knock on, through port 443. Its pod is called gate and it holds the front door (nginx), the bouncer (Authelia), the vault and the admin toys. World 2, The Stream, is the SGIN mini PC cobra-stream at 10.6.9.20 with the drives and the Intel GPU. Its pod is called stream and it holds the Cinema, the four Navidromes, the Library, the Comics shop, the Arcade and the Radio.

      
Every chapter follows the same game plan. The opener is the character card: what it is, where it lives, its port, its folders, how hard it hits. Then How it works, with a level map of where your request travels. Then Tips & tricks for things you can do with it today. Last, the Joe Sabotage Report: the traps we actually stepped on while building this place, and the ones the manual warns about, each with its fix.

      
The commands in the green CODES boxes are one per line, bare, ready to copy. Above each one, a yellow tag says which machine and which user to run it as, because "the-box first, always" is the law of this island.

    

    

      
PRO TIP

        
The gate runs the manual's Quadlet files (~/.config/containers/systemd/ as podsvc). So on the gate, restart an app with systemctl --user restart <name>, not the ./name.sh scripts. The scripts refuse to fight a Quadlet anyway.

BEFORE YOU PLAY

    
Two users, two jobs. eels is you, with sudo, for anything about the machine itself (fstab, nftables, packages). podsvc owns every container and has no sudo. Get a real podsvc session with sudo machinectl shell podsvc@; sudo su podsvc and sudo -u podsvc leave out the systemd user session that rootless podman needs, so things fail in confusing ways.
