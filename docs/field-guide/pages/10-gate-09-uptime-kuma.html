Uptime Kuma is the island's sentry. Every minute or so it knocks on every app, every router and both Pi-holes. When one doesn't answer, it turns the monitor red and sends you a notification. When it comes back, it tells you that too.

It lives in the gate pod on 127.0.0.1:3001, behind Authelia's two-factor sign-in at status.songofeo.com, with its own admin login on top. Version 2 (the image is pinned to uptime-kuma:2). Everything it knows, monitors, history and settings, is in /srv/gate/uptime-kuma.

status. has no public DNS record: from outside the house the name doesn't resolve at all. At home and over WireGuard the Pi-holes answer it, so you check it from THE-BOX or from the phone with WireGuard on.


        
CLAUDE-VIPERHomepage's dots tell you when you look. Kuma tells you when you don't. That's the difference between a dashboard and an alarm.

WHY PUBLIC NAMES WORK

        
The gate's DNS servers are the two Pi-holes (set at install: 10.6.9.3 and 10.6.9.20). They answer every *.songofeo.com name with 10.6.9.10, so Kuma checking https://tv.songofeo.com goes through the gate's own nginx to the app. One monitor tests the certificate, nginx and the app together.

      

      
HEARTBEATS

        
Each check is a "heartbeat": green bar for up, red for down. Kuma keeps the history, so you can see when something has been flaky all week, not just whether it's up right now.

      

      
WEBSOCKETS

        
The dashboard updates live over a websocket. nginx keeps it open for an hour (proxy_read_timeout 1h in the admin block).

      

    

    

      
THE PROTECTED ROOMS

        
A check on papers. from the gate gets Authelia's redirect to the sign-in, not Paperless. That still proves nginx and Authelia are up. To check the app behind it, point a monitor at its back door instead, like http://127.0.0.1:8000: Kuma is in the same pod.

      

      
NOTIFICATIONS

        
Settings, Notifications: Kuma can send to email and many phone and chat services. Make one, then attach it to every monitor (there's a "default enabled" switch so new monitors get it too).

      

      
VERSION 2

        
Uptime Kuma 2.0 went stable in October 2025. The image tag :2 keeps you on 2.x updates without jumping to a future 3.

CLAUDE-VIPERIf BOTH Pi-holes go down, every public-name monitor turns red at once, even though the apps are fine. A wall of red usually means DNS, not twenty broken apps.

  

  
PRO TIP: RESPONSE TIMES

    
Each HTTP monitor also graphs how long the app took to answer. A slowly climbing line warns you before the red ever shows up.

  






  
TIPS & TRICKSPOWER-UPS

  

    

      
PRO TIP: CLAIM IT FIRST

        
Kuma's very first visit asks you to create the admin account. Do it on THE-BOX right away, with a generated password saved in Vaultwarden.

      

      
PRO TIP: MONITOR GROUPS

        
Group monitors as World 1 (gate apps), World 2 (SGIN apps) and Network (Proxmox, OPNsense, Pi-holes). A whole group going red points straight at the machine.

      

      
PRO TIP: KEYWORD CHECKS

        
An HTTP monitor can also look for a word on the page. Checking that tv. contains "Jellyfin" catches the case where nginx answers but serves the wrong thing.

      

      
PRO TIP: THE HISCORE BEAT

        
When the hiscore board is live, add an HTTP monitor for http://127.0.0.1:8090/healthz. Same pod, so no sign-in in the way.

      

    

    

      
PRO TIP: QUIET HOURS

        
Planning to reboot the SGIN? Pause its monitors, or set a maintenance window, so your phone doesn't buzz for every app on it.

      

      
PRO TIP: CERT WATCH

        
HTTP monitors can warn before a certificate expires. Turn it on for one public name: the wildcard cert covers them all.

JOE SABOTAGE REPORT

      
The sentry can be fooled. Here's how.

    

  

  

    

      
THE UNCLAIMED THRONE

        
Until someone creates Kuma's admin, the first visitor who reaches it gets to. Authelia's TOTP keeps strangers out, but don't leave it unclaimed.Claim it on THE-BOX on the first visit (on the 6 Nov to-do list).

      

      
THE DNS AVALANCHE

        
Both Pi-holes down: every public-name monitor fails together.Look at the two DNS monitors first. If they're red, fix DNS before anything else.

      

      
THE SILENT ALARM

        
Monitors with no notification attached go red where nobody sees them.Settings, Notifications, then attach to every monitor and tick "default enabled".

      

    

    

      
THE SELF-SIGNED RED

        
Proxmox, OPNsense and PBS use self-signed certificates, so a normal HTTP check always fails.Tick "Ignore TLS/SSL errors" on those three only.

      

      
THE WATCHER'S BLIND SPOT

        
Kuma lives on the gate. If the gate itself is down, Kuma is down too, and can't tell you.Proxmox's own notifications (manual 5.7, SMTP) cover the host. An outside check would be the next step.

      

      
THE OUTSIDE LOOK

        
status. has no public record, so it won't load on cellular without WireGuard.Turn WireGuard on, or check from THE-BOX.

      

    

  

    

    
THE SIGN-IN SMILE

      
A check on papers. follows Authelia's redirect and reads green even if Paperless is down.For admin apps, also watch the back door on 127.0.0.1.

    

    
THE TOO-EAGER BELL

      
Checks every 20 seconds with no retries buzz your phone for every hiccup.Give monitors a retry or two before they count as down.
